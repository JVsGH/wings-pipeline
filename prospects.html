Prospect production

Every player whose rights Detroit holds, with this season's scoring in each league "translated" to an NHL-equivalent pace (NHLe).

NHLe / 82 = points per game × league factor × 82. It estimates how many points a player would score over a full NHL season at the same rate. Factors live in data/nhle_factors.json. * = fewer than 10 GP, so treat the number with caution. Leagues without a factor (international tournaments) show no NHLe. Click a column header to sort (on smaller screens, use the sort menu above the list).
