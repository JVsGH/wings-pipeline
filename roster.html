Organization roster

Every player whose NHL rights Detroit holds, wherever they're playing this season, from the Red Wings and Griffins to college, junior and Europe. A player who has moved between leagues appears under each one. Each table shows every stat the NHL publishes for that league.

Regular season only. Tables open in a compact view; Show all columns adds every stat (scroll sideways if your screen is narrow). Click a column to sort. P/G, G/G, A/G: per game. NHLe: points (goals, assists) over 82 NHL games at this pace, using each league's factor. NHL only: P/60, G/60 per 60 minutes of ice time; S/GP shots per game; EVP even-strength points; PP% share of points on the power play; GSAA goals saved above a league-average goalie on the same shots. Prospect marks players on the Prospects page (linked to their profile here); everyone else links to NHL.com. EP opens a search on Elite Prospects. Players on AHL- or ECHL-only contracts aren't Detroit property and aren't listed.
