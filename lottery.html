Mock Draft Lottery

How it works: the 16 non-playoff teams are seeded worst to best by points percentage. The first draw decides #1 and the second decides #2. A winner can move up at most 10 spots: if a team seeded 12th or lower wins the first draw, it moves up 10 and the worst team keeps #1. In the second draw, any team already holding a top pick is redrawn, and the 10-spot limit counts from where a team sits after the first draw (so a team pushed down by a first-draw jump has a shorter reach). Everyone else drops into the remaining spots in standings order, so the worst team picks no lower than 3rd. The odds and the 10-spot cap match the NHL's current rules; the "Lottery odds" tab shows exact probabilities from every possible pair of draws. Traded picks and their protections update automatically every day . A protected pick shows the team that actually holds it at that slot; hover a team for trade details. Record and Pts% always describe the team whose finish sets the pick; when another club holds it, they carry that original team's tag (e.g. FLA .667). Not modeled: the rule that limits how often one team can win the lottery.
