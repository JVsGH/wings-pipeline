Detroit's pipeline runs through Grand Rapids, the NCAA, the CHL, Sweden, Russia and beyond. Mainstream coverage checks in a few times a season. This site follows those players **game by game**.

## What you'll find here

- **Recaps:** what a prospect did last night, beyond the box score.
- **Analysis:** trends, usage, and how the numbers compare.
- **Opinion:** takes, rankings and arguments.
- **Scouting:** film-based notes on skills and habits.
- **Draft:** players Detroit could target at the next draft.

## About NHLe

The [Prospects](prospects.html) table "translates" scoring from each league into an NHL-equivalent pace. It takes points per game, multiplies by a league factor, and scales to 82 games. A 1.00 P/GP season in the OHL (factor 0.14) translates to about 11 NHL points over 82 games. The same pace in the AHL (0.39) translates to about 32.

Treat NHLe as context, not destiny. Small samples swing wildly, age matters a lot (a 17-year-old and a 20-year-old with the same pace are very different prospects), and it says nothing about defense or goaltending.
